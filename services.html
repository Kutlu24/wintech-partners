IT Support & Maintenance

  • Troubleshooting

    Fast, efficient resolution of hardware and software problems, to keep your downtime to a minimum.

  • Device Setup & Installation

    Professional setup of new computers, printers, and network devices for a smooth start.

  • System Imaging & Recovery

    System images (e.g. via Acronis) for fast recovery and consistent configurations across your machines.

  • RAM & Hardware Upgrades

    More life out of your existing hardware through targeted upgrades - memory, SSDs, and more.

Cybersecurity

    Setup and configuration of security firewalls to protect your whole network from outside threats.

    Automated backup strategies (local and cloud) to protect you against data loss.

  • Data Encryption

    Advice on and implementation of encryption (e.g. BitLocker, VeraCrypt) for sensitive data.

Digitalization & Automation

  • Paperless Processes

    Orders, forms, approvals and invoices turned into structured digital workflows your team actually uses - no more print, scan, retype.

  • Workflow Automation

    Repetitive steps automated with the tools you already have: reminders, approvals, notifications and recurring reports (Microsoft 365, SharePoint, Power Automate).

  • Tool Selection & Integration

    Vendor-neutral choice of CRM, ERP and collaboration tools - and connecting them so your data is entered once instead of three times.

  • Rollout & Team Training

    New tools introduced step by step, with short practical training and documentation your team can look things up in.

Customer Service Chatbot

  • Website & Messenger Assistant

    A chatbot on your website, WhatsApp or email that answers common questions in German, English or French - day and night.

  • Triage & Lead Capture

    It sorts incoming requests, collects contact details and passes everything to a person with the full context, so no enquiry is lost.

  • Knowledge Base & FAQ

    Your answers, prices and opening hours live in one maintained knowledge base the bot answers from - factual, current, no invented content.

  • Data Protection by Design

    Swiss and EU data protection built in: your customer conversations are never used for training, and visitors are told they are talking to a bot.
